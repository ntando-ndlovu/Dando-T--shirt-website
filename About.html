About DANDO Studio

DANDO Studio is a South African clothing brand specialising in modern, comfortable and stylish T-shirts.

The brand was created around the idea that everyday clothing does not have to be complicated to look good.

Our Story

DANDO Studio was established as a small clothing brand inspired by modern streetwear and casual fashion trends.

The brand focuses on creating simple but eye-catching T-shirts that can be worn by young people in their everyday lives.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide stylish, comfortable and affordable T-shirts that allow customers to express their individual style confidently.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a recognisable South African streetwear brand known for simple designs, quality clothing and a modern online shopping experience.

Our Values

  • Comfort
  • Quality
  • Affordable fashion
  • Individual expression
  • Simple and modern design
